How it works

  1. 1
    Add one or two photos. Each is cropped to its frame window from the original pixels, never stretched.
  2. 2
    Tap a print to edit it. Drag, pinch and twist text, the date, emoji and stickers right on the print; drag one to the bin to delete it. Pinch or drag the photo to crop it. Layout shows lines you drag to size the photo.
  3. 3
    Print and cut. Export the PNG, print it from the SELPHY app, then cut down the middle for two Instax-style prints.

Nothing is uploaded. Photos, edits and themes are saved in this browser only.
