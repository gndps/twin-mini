How it works

  1. 1
    Add one or two photos. Each is cropped to the Instax window (46 : 62) without stretching, at its full original resolution. Phone orientation is respected; rotate any photo in 90° steps.
  2. 2
    Pick a frame. True Mini matches real film: 54 × 86 mm card, 46 × 62 mm photo, thick chin at the bottom. Both frames sit side by side on one 148 × 100 mm SELPHY postcard.
  3. 3
    Print, cut, trim. Print borderless, cut down the dashed line, then trim at the corner ticks.

Nothing is uploaded. Your photos never leave this device; all rendering happens in the browser.

Printing on the SELPHY CP1500

  • Paper. Use Postcard size (KP-108IN, 100 × 148 mm). The file is laid out at exactly that size, so nothing is stretched.
  • Borderless. Choose borderless. The printer trims a hair from every edge; the frames sit well inside that, and the cut line stays centred because the trim is symmetrical. Turn on Safe area to see it.
  • Turn off auto-correction. Disable “Image optimize”/auto correction and red-eye in the app so frame colors stay true.
  • Resolution. The CP1500 prints at 300 dpi. Auto keeps every pixel of your sharpest photo (up to 1200 dpi) so the printer does the final, single resample.
  • From a computer. Print at 100% / “actual size” on Postcard paper, borderless.
