How it works

  1. 1
    Add one or two photos. Each is cropped to the Instax window (46 : 62) from its full original resolution, never stretched. Phone orientation is respected; rotate any photo in 90° steps.
  2. 2
    Pick a frame colour and, if you like, write a caption on the chin. Everything else lives in Advanced.
  3. 3
    Print and cut. Print borderless on a SELPHY postcard and cut down the middle: two Instax-style prints.

Nothing is uploaded. Your photos never leave this device.

Printing tips

  • Paper. Postcard size (KP-108IN, 100 × 148 mm). The file is laid out at exactly that size.
  • Borderless. The printer trims about 2 mm from every edge. Half sheet already places the photo inside that, so borders come out even.
  • No auto-correction. Turn off “Image optimize” and red-eye correction in the SELPHY app so frame colours stay true.
  • Resolution. The CP1500 prints at 300 dpi. Auto keeps every pixel of your sharpest photo (up to 1200 dpi) so the printer does a single, final resample.
  • From a computer. Print at actual size on Postcard paper, borderless.
