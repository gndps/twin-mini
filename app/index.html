How it works

  1. 1
    Add one or two photos. Each is cropped to the Instax window (46 : 62) from its original pixels, never stretched. Turn or flip any photo.
  2. 2
    Choose a frame colour and captions. The photo’s date is added by default; save your favourite caption styles as templates.
  3. 3
    Print and cut. Print the PNG from the SELPHY app, then cut down the middle for two Instax-style prints.

Nothing is uploaded. Photos stay on this device; settings and templates are saved in this browser.
